
## How to work: one theory, one change
Before every `./check`, add one line to `notes.md`: what differs (from the last check), your theory why, and the
single change you are making to test it. Change one thing at a time, so each result tells you something. Every check
saves a snapshot in `.attempts/` and tells you the best one so far: if a change makes the score worse, go back to the
best version (copy it to attempt.c) before trying the next idea. Don't repeat a change you already tried.
